# Archive — evicted and refuted cards

Cards land here, never in `/dev/null`. Two ways in:

- **Refuted** — new evidence contradicted the claim. The entry keeps the refuting source, so the same
  idea does not get rediscovered and re-inserted next quarter.
- **Evicted** — `INDEX.md` hit its 40-line cap and this card had the lowest `confidence × freshness`.
  Eviction is not a judgement about truth; a ★★★ card is never auto-evicted.

An archived card is still readable evidence. It is simply no longer surfaced to a running workflow.
