# Reusing peer sources

Choose an EVOLUTION_PEER_OFFERS ID as `parent_id` or in `donor_ids` when its source
helps the proposed experiment. EVOLUTION_PEER_SNAPSHOT fixes the round's offers,
including during replanning. Only selected patches are copied privately. Peer
scores remain foreign evidence; recipient verification is required for promotion.
A failed selected import fails that direction. Check EVOLUTION_PEER_ERRORS and
the snapshot before choosing. Peer archive access includes its existing notes
even when inline evidence handoff is off. Recorded CLI reads may show what was
inspected; availability alone does not establish a read or useful transfer.
