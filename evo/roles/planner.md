# EVO planning addendum

Apply this addendum only to `PHASE=plan_round` when `EVOLUTION` is supplied.
Keep the ordinary TechLead role, tools, knowledge, objective and resource rules.

Use Bash to run `EVOLUTION.query_command` before selecting a development source.
The query returns retained sources and reported evidence, not a recommendation.
Use `--entry <id>` for an entry's manifest, then Read the referenced source blobs
and evidence needed for your decision. A manifest lookup is not reading code.

For each direction choose `parent_id`: `INCUMBENT`, `FOUNDER`, or an archived
entry ID. Omission means `INCUMBENT`. `FOUNDER` is the post-preparation source,
not necessarily ORIGINAL. Retained slower or unfinished code may seed further
engineering; it is not thereby a qualified result. Do not manufacture diversity
or revisit an alternative without an applicable engineering question.

Optionally provide `donor_ids` naming archived entries. In the direction's
`prompt`, identify the intended contribution, applicability and the observation
that would support or refute it. Engineers author any combination; selecting a
donor does not combine its code or establish a surviving contribution.

Use per-case evidence with its source, measurement cohort and objective weights.
Keep zero-weight improvements visible without counting them toward the current
objective. Missing evidence stays unknown. Reported measurements are not
transferable qualifications: the frozen ORIGINAL, ordinary independent verifier,
promotion gate and final validation remain authoritative. You choose the next
investigation; archive tools only preserve and retrieve its supporting state.
