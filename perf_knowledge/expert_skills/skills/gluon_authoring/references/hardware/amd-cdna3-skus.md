# AMD CDNA3 — SKU planning peaks (gfx942)

**Planning models, not sustained rates.** Calibrate from the anchor trace (`roofline-models.md`
§Calibration). Microarchitecture (LDS, VGPR, occupancy): `planning-constants.md`. Lever gate:
`capability-matrix.md`.

```text
saturation = grid_tiles / CUs          # <1 critical; 1–2 under-utilized
L2_MB      = per-GPU L2 (layer 6 locality; not HBM roofline)
```

**This `saturation` is the coarse fill check, not the quantization tail.** It answers *does the
grid cover the device at all*, and it is **not** `tail_efficiency` — it says nothing about how
much of the *last* wave is wasted. That quantity is `grid_tiles / (waves * CUs)`; mechanism in
`roofline-models.md ## Saturation / wave quantization`, and the rule that it is one-sided — it
can veto a geometry but **cannot rank two configurations** — in
`planning-constants.md ## Tail efficiency is one-sided too`.

Peak dtype: **Matrix MFMA** for GEMM roofline; **FP32 vector** for epilogue / VALU feeds-and-speeds.

## Quick reference

**gfx942 is the downgrade line** (gfx950 / `amd-cdna4-skus.md` is the main one). The peak rows are
rendered from `perf_knowledge/hardware/data/sku.json` (`extract_sku.py --sync-docs`) — edit the
json, never these cells. Dense, no sparsity (MI300X FP16 with 2:4 sparsity is 2614.9 TF and is NOT
a dense peak).
