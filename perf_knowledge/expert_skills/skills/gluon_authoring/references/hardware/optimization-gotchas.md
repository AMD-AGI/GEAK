# Optimization gotchas (the reversed-intuition facts)

Profile-reading traps where the default move is wrong on gfx950 (gfx942 notes at the end). Detail
is in the linked file.

| # | Misread | Discriminator | What the signal points to | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low `MfmaUtil` -> "occupancy wall" | **Occupancy adequacy**: >= 2 waves AND no VGPR/LDS limiter | Adequate -> points to an under-filled tile (growing it is the test); occupancy relief fits when waves < 2 or a limiter caps it | `bound-class-signals.md ## under-fill vs occupancy` |
| 2 | Reading a scaled-MFMA (fp8/fp4) kernel's matrix counters with the wrong op counter | `MfmaUtil` / `SQ_VALU_MFMA_BUSY_CYCLES` count scaled MFMA on gfx950 (rocprofv3 1.3.5); the op counters split by format: fp8 x fp4 -> `SQ_INSTS_VALU_MFMA_MOPS_F8`, fp4 x fp4 -> `SQ_INSTS_VALU_MFMA_MOPS_F6F4` | Read busy cycles for engagement and the op counter matching the operand formats issued | `bound-class-signals.md ## Rules` |
| 3 | L2 hit low -> XCD / `GROUP_SIZE_M` remap | L2-low + coalescing-low is a symptom of the access pattern | Coalescing / tile size is the stronger hypothesis; a remap is the stronger one when access is already coalesced (a remap has also paid on its own on an unchanged body - A/B) | `bound-class-signals.md` |
| 4 | Full-drain `lgkmcnt(0)` + low `MfmaUtil` -> "bandwidth-bound" | At equal VGPR+AGPR and occupancy vs plain, with `MemUnitStalled` / stalled-on-L2 not dominant, it is a **schedule / overlap gap** | Hand-written overlap options: register prefetch, authored LDS ring (gfx950 `async_copy` + `commit_group` / `wait_group`), `warp_pipeline_stage` (`num_warps>=8`), the per-compile scheduler strategy; which comes first depends on the tile (register-resident / few-MFMA loops vs LDS-staged compute-bound tiles). Re-injecting plain's pipeliner is a diagnostic (`injected`), not counted as a win | `../tile-programming/pipeline.md` |
| 5 | Low-precision GEMM at "N% of MFMA roofline" -> chase MFMA continuity | `asm_loop_audit` VALU% >> MFMA%: a per-element convert on the operand path (int4/int8 unpack, or the common **fp8 -> bf16 upcast feeding regular MFMA**) | VALU / software-dequant: the convert is the cost (a native converter, or native scaled MFMA so operands do not widen, removes or cheapens it). The upcast form also flips row 2 | `../tile-programming/low-precision.md` |
| 6 | A latency-hiding / prefetch lever "should help" | Occupancy gates it: pays most reliably at low occupancy; with 2+ resident waves often neutral-to-negative, with measured exceptions where the removed latency sat on the MFMA dependency chain | Bounding the win (disable the targeted dependency, measure) sizes it before the build | `bound-class-signals.md ## Lever gating laws` |
| 7 | A GEMM throughput-pairing scheduler / AGPR accumulator hint as "just another knob" | It assumes a pure MFMA -> MFMA accumulator chain; on VALU-between-matmul loops the gfx950-tutorial-v0.1 fork pass emitted invalid IR ("does not dominate all uses") on attention and crashed or slowed on block-scale (rebuilt and reproduced); later versions of that pass verify and roll back (a silent no-op), and upstream MFMA scheduling (v3.1 / main) leaves such loops alone. The AGPR accumulator hint works against loops whose VALU reads the accumulator every iteration (attention rescale, per-block scaling) but helped operand-side dequant by removing spills | Decide by toolchain version and accumulator read cadence; check the ISA for the schedule and spills before attributing a flat result; interleave by hand on attention-class loops. Stock `coexec` is gfx1250-only on 3.8.0 (no route on gfx950/gfx942, source-checked) - manual interleave is the remaining route | `../tile-programming/compiler-contract.md` |
| 8 | A compiler pass lever regressed -> "compiler ceiling" | A compiler-realized lever exploits existing structure; it does not create it | Check the IR has the paired structure (independent load / LDS read / dot, fitting registers) before blaming the pass | `../tile-programming/compiler-contract.md` |
| 9 | `s_nop` in the hot loop -> "exposed hazard, unroll" | Three origins: (a) compiler hazard NOP; (b) deliberate stage-head phase shift (register-file port contention with 3-source VALU); (c) ISA-mandated wait state before DPP / `permlane` / cross-lane ops - look at the **next** instruction | (a) unroll / occupancy; (b) sweep, non-monotone; (c) **deleting it is a correctness bug** (`amd_isa.py --arch gfx950 waits`) | `isa-mechanisms.md ## Instruction-rate facts that flip a lever's sign` |
| 10 | A schedule pass changed nothing -> "schedule ceiling" | An unmodelled MFMA shape or an unsatisfiable declaration can make the pass skip the region silently (plugin-version dependent: `capability-matrix.md`) | Confirm the pass acted — scheduler markers or an ISA diff against the hint-off build — before recording a ceiling | `../tile-programming/llir-codesign.md` |

gfx942: row 2 does not arise (fp8 is FNUZ, non-scaled); row 4's ring downgrades to sync staging;
read `cdna3-gfx942.md` before trusting a gfx950-calibrated reading.
